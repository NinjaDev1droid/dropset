## 1. Getting Started — First Time Setup

Before you use any tool, do this once. It takes 5 minutes.

### Step 1 — Open the app
1. Go into your `DJ_Setlist` folder on your computer
2. Open the `afroflow` folder
3. Double-click `index.html`
4. It opens in your browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari — all work)

### Step 2 — Load your music library
1. From the home page, click **Library Manager**
2. Click the **⬇️ Import DropSet DB** button
3. This loads 50+ pre-tagged Afrobeats, Hip-Hop, R&B, Dancehall, and Latin tracks instantly
4. Your library is now ready — add your own personal tracks on top of it

### Step 3 — Set up your EPK
1. From the home page, click **DJ Press Kit (EPK)**
2. Click **✨ Start** and fill in the form — name, photo, bio, mixes, contacts
3. Your page builds itself as you type and saves automatically
4. This takes 10 minutes and you only do it once (update it whenever something new happens)

### Step 4 — Add your first gig
1. From the home page, click **Gig Tracker**
2. Click **+ Add Gig** and log your next upcoming booking
3. Even if it's unpaid or pending — log it now so you track everything

You're set up. Now let's go deep on each tool.

## 2. Tool 1 — Setlist Generator 🎵

**What it does:** Builds a full setlist for any event in seconds based on your crowd, vibe, and duration.

**Open it:** Home → Setlist Generator

---

### How to build a setlist

**Step 1 — Choose your Event Type**

Pick what kind of gig you're playing:

| Event Type | When to pick it |
|---|---|
| Club Night | Late night, dark room, peak hour crowd |
| Wedding / Private Party | Mixed age crowd, needs to please everyone |
| Festival / Outdoor | Big space, lots of people, long sets |
| Lounge / Rooftop | Chill vibe, background music, conversation happening |
| Birthday Party | Fun, celebratory, crowd knows each other |
| Corporate Event | Professional, moderate energy, safe picks |

---

**Step 2 — Set your Duration**

Pick how long your set is (1 hour to 4 hours). The tool automatically calculates how many tracks you need — roughly one track every 4 minutes.

---

**Step 3 — Pick your Crowd Vibe**

This is the most important setting. It controls the energy curve of your entire set:

| Vibe | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Hype — Full energy, peak hour** | Starts strong, builds to maximum. Best for late-night club sets |
| **Mixed — Build up and release** | Starts medium, peaks in the middle, winds down at the end. Best for parties and weddings |
| **Smooth — Chill, background vibes** | Stays low energy throughout. Best for lounges and corporate events |
| **Dance floor — Non-stop movement** | High and consistent the whole way. Best for festivals |

---

**Step 4 — Choose your Genre**

Pick the music style that fits your crowd. You have 5 groups to choose from:

**🌍 African:**
- **Full African Mix** — blends everything (Afrobeats + Amapiano + Afro House + Afro-Pop). Best all-round pick for African events.
- **Afrobeats** — Nigerian sound. Burna Boy, Wizkid, Davido, Rema, Asake.
- **Amapiano** — South African sound. Kabza De Small, DJ Maphorisa, Focalistic.
- **Afro House** — Deep, electronic African sound. Black Coffee, Shimza.
- **Afro-Pop** — Mainstream African pop, crowd pleasers.

**🎤 Hip-Hop:**
- **All Hip-Hop** — mixes all sub-genres automatically.
- **Trap** — Travis Scott, Migos, Future, Post Malone.
- **Old School / Golden Age** — 2Pac, Biggie, Eminem, OutKast, Nas.
- **Drill** — Pop Smoke, Central Cee, Juice WRLD.
- **Conscious Hip-Hop** — Kendrick Lamar, J. Cole, Common.

**🎵 R&B & Soul:**
- **All R&B & Soul** — mixes all sub-genres.
- **Contemporary R&B** — Beyoncé, H.E.R., Silk Sonic, Alicia Keys.
- **Neo-Soul** — Erykah Badu, D'Angelo, Jill Scott.
- **Quiet Storm** — Sade, Luther Vandross, Marvin Gaye. Best for mature, intimate events.
- **Motown** — Jackson 5, Supremes, Stevie Wonder. Works for every crowd.

**🌴 Reggae & Latin:**
- **All Reggae & Latin** — mixes everything.
- **Reggaeton** — Daddy Yankee, Bad Bunny, J Balvin.
- **Salsa** — Marc Anthony, Celia Cruz. Great for Latin events.
- **Samba** — Brazilian sound, upbeat and fun.
- **Dancehall** — Sean Paul, Vybz Kartel, Rihanna.

**🔀 Everything** — pulls from all genres at once. Perfect for a diverse crowd with no single cultural focus.

---

**Step 5 — Set Crowd Age Group**

| Age Group | Best for |
|---|---|
| Young (18–25) | Hype crowd, current hits, trap, drill, new Afrobeats |
| Mixed (25–40) | Most parties — this is the safest default |
| Mature (35+) | Old school, Quiet Storm, Motown, smooth R&B |

---

**Step 6 — Optional: Lock your opener and closer**

If you already know exactly what song you want to start and end with, type it in. Format: `Artist – Song Title` (example: `Burna Boy – Last Last`). The generator will lock those in and fill everything in between.

---

**Step 7 — Generate and read your setlist**

Click **🎧 Generate My Setlist**. You'll see:

- **Energy Curve** — a colored bar showing how your set's energy flows from start to end
- **Track table** — every song with BPM, energy level, and DJ notes (e.g. "🔥 Peak moment — drop it!")
- **Color coding** — blue = low, green = medium, orange = high, red = peak

---

**Step 8 — Save or print your setlist**

- Click **📋 Copy to Clipboard** to paste it into Notes, WhatsApp, or any app
- Click **🖨️ Print** to get a physical copy for your DJ booth
- Click **🔄 Generate Another** to get a different set with the same settings

---

**Pro tips for the Setlist Generator:**
- Run it 2–3 times and pick the version you like best — every run shuffles differently
- For a wedding: use "Mixed" vibe + "Mature" crowd + "Full African Mix" or "Motown"
- For a club: use "Hype" vibe + "Young" crowd + "Trap" or "Full African Mix"
- For a corporate event: use "Smooth" vibe + "Mixed" crowd + "Quiet Storm" or "Neo-Soul"

## 3. Tool 2 — Library Manager 📚

**What it does:** Your personal track database. Store every song you own with BPM, key, energy, vibe tags, and notes. Search and filter in seconds.

**Open it:** Home → Library Manager

---

### How to build your library

**Step 1 — Import the DropSet database first**

Click **⬇️ Import DropSet DB** — this loads 50+ pre-built tracks across all genres instantly. It's a starting point. You'll add your own on top.

---

**Step 2 — Add your own tracks**

Click **+ Add Track** and fill in:

| Field | What to put |
|---|---|
| **Song Title** | The track name |
| **Artist** | Main artist (and featured artist if applicable) |
| **Genre** | Pick from the dropdown — African, Hip-Hop, R&B, Latin etc. |
| **BPM** | Beats per minute — check your DJ software (Rekordbox, Serato, Traktor) |
| **Key** | Musical key — also from your DJ software |
| **Energy Level** | Drag the slider: 1–3 = chill, 4–6 = medium, 7–8 = high, 9–10 = peak |
| **Vibe** | Check all that apply: Hype, Dance Floor, Mixed, Smooth |
| **Crowd** | Who this song works for: Young, Mixed, Mature |
| **Notes** | Anything useful — "opens sets well", "crowd always reacts", "transitions into Essence" |
| **Link** | SoundCloud, YouTube, or Beatport link for quick access |

Click **Save Track**. Done.

---

**Step 3 — Search and filter your library**

Use the bar at the top to:

- **Search** — type any word (artist name, song title, genre, your own notes)
- **Filter by Genre** — show only Afrobeats, or only Trap, etc.
- **Filter by Vibe** — show only Hype tracks, or only Smooth tracks
- **Filter by Energy** — Low (1–3), Medium (4–6), High (7–8), Peak (9–10)
- **Sort** — by date added, title, artist, BPM, or energy

---

**Step 4 — Edit a track**

Click the ✏️ button on any track to update any field. Do this whenever you learn more about a song — better BPM reading, a new tag, a note about how the crowd reacted.

---

**Step 5 — Export your library**

Click **📤 Export CSV** to download your entire library as a spreadsheet. You can open it in Excel or Google Sheets, sort it any way you want, or share it with another DJ.

---

**What to track in your Notes field (pro habit):**

- How the crowd reacted: `"Dancefloor filled up immediately"`
- What it transitions well into: `"Goes perfectly into Calm Down"`
- Context where it works: `"Best for warm-up, not peak hour"`
- Red flags: `"Crowd slowed down, use carefully"`

Over time your notes become your most valuable DJ asset — personal experience no one else has.

## 4. Tool 3 — DJ Press Kit (EPK) 🪪

**What it does:** A professional one-page DJ profile you can share with promoters, venues, and clients to get more bookings.

**Open it:** Home → DJ Press Kit (EPK)

---

### How to set up your EPK

The first time you open it, you'll see **Create your press kit**. Click **✨ Start**. A form opens on the right (full screen on a phone) and your page builds itself next to it as you type. Everything saves by itself — there's no save button. Skip anything you don't have yet; empty sections are simply hidden.

To change something later, click **✏️ Edit Press Kit** at the top. Click **✓ Done** (or press Esc) to close the form.

---

**Step 1 — The basics**

Add a photo (**📷 Add photo**), your DJ name, a one-line tagline and where you're based. Tagline examples:
- `Afrobeats · Amapiano · African Sounds — Worldwide`
- `Hip-Hop · R&B · Dancehall — From the Streets to the Stage`
- `All Genres, One Vibe — Available for Events Worldwide`

---

**Step 2 — Write your bio**

A good DJ bio answers:

1. Where are you from and what's your sound?
2. What makes you different from other DJs?
3. What kind of events do you play?
4. What's one impressive thing you've done?

Keep it to 4–6 sentences. Promoters don't read long bios.

---

**Step 3 — Your numbers**

| Stat | What to put |
|---|---|
| Events Played | Total gigs you've done in your career |
| Years Experience | How long you've been DJing professionally |
| Social Followers | Your Instagram or TikTok follower count |
| Countries Performed | Number of countries you've played in |

You can rename any of them. Leave a number empty to hide it. Small and real is better than big and fake.

---

**Step 4 — Genres and event types**

Tap the genres you play and the events you're available for. Missing one? Type it in the box underneath and press Enter.

---

**Step 5 — Add your mixes**

Click **+ Add a mix**, type the mix name and paste its link. Mixcloud, SoundCloud and YouTube links turn into a player on your page, so promoters can listen without leaving it.

---

**Step 6 — YouTube**

Paste your channel link, then **+ Add a video** for each video you want to show. They appear as a grid of thumbnails.

---

**Step 7 — Past venues and quotes**

Add your past gigs (**+ Add a venue**) — be specific: "Club XO – London, 2025" looks more professional than "Club". Then add what promoters or clients have said about you (**+ Add a quote**). No quotes yet? Leave it empty — the section won't show.

---

**Step 8 — Booking contacts**

Fill in the ways people can book you: email, phone, WhatsApp (with country code), Instagram, TikTok, SoundCloud, Mixcloud, website. Only the ones you fill in are shown. If you add an email, your page gets a big **📩 Send Booking Request** button.

---

**Step 9 — Keep it safe**

Your press kit is saved in this browser. Go to DropSet Home → **💾 Your Data** → **⬇️ Download Backup** to keep a copy (it includes your press kit, library and gigs).

---

**Step 10 — Share your EPK**

To send it to a promoter or venue:

1. Open your EPK and click **🖨️ Save as PDF** at the top
2. Change destination to "Save as PDF" (buttons, the edit form and music players are left out automatically)
3. Save it and send the PDF file via WhatsApp, email, or DM

## 5. Tool 4 — Gig Tracker 📅

**What it does:** Logs every booking, tracks your income, monitors payment status, and generates social media captions after every event.

**Open it:** Home → Gig Tracker

---

### How to track your gigs

**Step 1 — Add every gig, even future ones**

Click **+ Add Gig** as soon as a booking is confirmed — don't wait until after the event. Fill in:

| Field | What to put |
|---|---|
| **Venue / Event Name** | Club name, event name, or "Private Party – [Client]" |
| **City / Location** | City and country if international |
| **Date** | Pick from the calendar |
| **Start Time** | What time your set starts |
| **Set Duration** | How long you play |
| **Event Type** | Club Night, Wedding, Festival, etc. |
| **Status** | Confirmed, Pending, Completed, or Cancelled |
| **Payment Amount** | What you're being paid in dollars |
| **Payment Status** | Unpaid, Deposit Received, or Fully Paid |
| **Client / Promoter Name** | Who booked you |
| **Client Contact** | Their phone or email |
| **Notes** | Setup requirements, travel info, special requests |
| **Setlist Used** | Optional — link it to a setlist you generated |

---

**Step 2 — Read your income dashboard**

At the top of the page you'll always see:

| Card | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **Total Gigs** | Every gig ever logged |
| **Completed** | Gigs that are done |
| **Upcoming** | Gigs confirmed for the future |
| **Total Earned** | Money from completed + paid gigs |
| **Avg Per Gig** | Your average fee — use this when negotiating |
| **Pending Payment** | Money people still owe you |

---

**Step 3 — Update status after every gig**

When a gig is done:
1. Click the ✏️ edit button on that gig
2. Change Status from "Confirmed" → "Completed"
3. Update Payment Status to "Fully Paid" once you receive the money
4. Add any notes about how the night went

This keeps your dashboard accurate.

---

**Step 4 — Generate your social media caption**

Right after a gig, click the **📱 button** on that gig card. You get 4 caption styles to choose from:

| Style | Best for |
|---|---|
| **Hype 🔥** | Instagram/TikTok — young, energetic audience |
| **Professional 🎯** | LinkedIn or formal promoter posts |
| **Story 📖** | Instagram captions that tell a narrative |
| **Minimal ✨** | Clean, aesthetic posts — less text, more vibe |

Click **🔄 Regenerate** to get a fresh version of the same style. Click **📋 Copy** and paste straight into Instagram, TikTok, Twitter, or WhatsApp.

---

**Step 5 — Filter and search your history**

Use the filters to find specific gigs:
- Search by venue, city, or client name
- Filter by status (Completed, Upcoming, etc.)
- Filter by event type (Weddings only, Clubs only, etc.)
- Filter by year to see all gigs from a specific year
- Sort by date or by highest-paying gig

---

**Step 6 — Export for tax records or invoicing**

Click **📤 Export CSV** to download your full gig history as a spreadsheet. This is useful for:
- Keeping records at tax time
- Showing promoters your history
- Sending to an accountant

## 6. How the Four Tools Work Together

DropSet is designed so the four tools feed into each other naturally.

```
LIBRARY MANAGER  ──→  SETLIST GENERATOR
   (your tracks)           (uses your tracks to build sets)
         │
         ↓
    GIG TRACKER    ──→  EPK (Press Kit)
   (logs the gig)        (shows your history to get more gigs)
         │
         ↓
   SOCIAL CAPTION
   (promote the gig after)
```

**The full cycle for one gig:**

1. **Library Manager** — your tracks are tagged and ready
2. **Setlist Generator** — you build the perfect set for this event
3. **Gig Tracker** — you log the booking and the setlist you used
4. **Play the gig**
5. **Gig Tracker** — you update status to Completed, generate a social caption, post it
6. **EPK** — update your past venues section with the new gig, increase your stats number
7. **Repeat** — your EPK gets stronger, your library grows, your income history builds

## 7. Weekly Routine — The DropSet Workflow

Follow this routine and DropSet does the work for you over time.

### Before a gig (1–2 days before)
- [ ] Open **Setlist Generator** — build 2–3 versions of your setlist
- [ ] Pick the best one and print it or copy it to your phone
- [ ] Check **Gig Tracker** — confirm the venue, time, and client contact are correct
- [ ] Double-check payment status — is the deposit received?

### After a gig (same night or next morning)
- [ ] Open **Gig Tracker** — change status to "Completed"
- [ ] Update payment status (Unpaid → Deposit Received → Fully Paid)
- [ ] Generate a **Social Media Caption** and post to Instagram/TikTok
- [ ] Add any notes about the crowd, what worked, what didn't

### Once a week (Sunday or Monday)
- [ ] Open **Library Manager** — add any new tracks you discovered that week
- [ ] Tag them properly (BPM, energy, vibe) — even 5 new tracks a week = 260 a year
- [ ] Review your **Gig Tracker** dashboard — check pending payments, upcoming gigs
- [ ] Contact anyone who still owes you money

### Once a month
- [ ] Open your **EPK** — update the stats (Events Played, Followers)
- [ ] Add any new venues to your Past Venues list
- [ ] Add any new testimonials from clients
- [ ] Export your gig history from **Gig Tracker** as a CSV backup
- [ ] Review your average pay per gig — if it hasn't gone up, it's time to raise your rate

## 8. Tips & Tricks

### Setlist Generator
- Always generate at least 3 setlists before a gig and pick the best one
- Use the "Everything" genre option for diverse international crowds
- If the crowd isn't reacting, switch vibes mentally — you already have an alternative setlist ready
- Print your setlist and keep it in your DJ bag every night

### Library Manager
- The more detail you add to Notes, the more valuable your library becomes over time
- Tag tracks immediately after playing them — your memory is sharpest right after the gig
- Use the Crowd filter before a gig to quickly see what tracks work for that night's audience
- Export your CSV monthly and save it as a backup — your library is stored in the browser

### DJ Press Kit (EPK)
- Update it every time something new happens — new venue, new follower milestone, new testimonial
- Send your EPK PDF before every new client conversation — it sets a professional tone
- Keep your bio honest and specific — "I've played 3 festivals in Lagos" beats "I play everywhere"
- A strong EPK gets you better gigs at better pay

### Gig Tracker
- Log every gig — even the small unpaid ones. Your total gig count is your credibility
- Check your "Pending Payment" number every week — follow up professionally until it's zero
- Use your Average Per Gig stat when someone tries to lowball you: "My average booking fee is $X"
- Post your social caption within 24 hours of the gig while the energy is fresh
